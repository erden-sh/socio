## Sign in and choose a small task

Use the sign-in method your team provides. An invitation or password link is personal: open it privately, choose your password if asked, and add a passkey under Account & security when available. If sign-in says you are not invited, ask an admin to check your account instead of creating another identity.

On the home screen or sidebar, choose a socio whose role fits your task. Open a new conversation and give it enough context to succeed: the outcome, relevant facts, constraints and what a useful answer looks like. For example: “Help me turn these notes into a short onboarding checklist. Ask about anything missing before drafting.”

Send the message yourself. A guide or tour explains controls; it does not send messages, save a page or approve actions for you. If Socio says no model is available, ask your operator about the shared model or check your own Inference connection. Repeatedly sending the same request does not repair a missing connection.

## Check the response

Read the result and inspect important facts. Ask for a correction or narrower answer before using it. Tool activity may show that the socio is reading a page, working with a file or waiting for approval. Open [Approvals](/docs/approvals) when the action needs your decision. You can stop a running turn; check the recorded outcome before repeating an external action.

## Open a page

Choose a [Space](/docs/spaces) and open a page from its library. You can edit a page directly without asking a model. Wait for the save status to confirm that the server has your changes. For help with the document, open its [page conversation](/docs/page-conversations) and choose a socio with access to that Space.

Pages are shared with the workspace; your conversation about a page is your own. Review [privacy and sharing](/docs/privacy) before copying private chat material into a page. Continue with [chats](/docs/chats), [pages](/docs/pages) and [socios](/docs/socios).
