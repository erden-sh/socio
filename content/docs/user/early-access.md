---
title: 'Early access'
description: 'Features your workspace tries before everyone gets them.'
---

Some features are ready to try before they are released to everyone. They carry an **Early access** badge, in the app and in What's new. They work, but they may still change or go away.

## Who gets them

Your workspace's admins decide, on **Settings → Early access**: on for the whole workspace, or for some roles and people. You can't turn an early-access feature on or off for yourself. If you'd like to try one, ask an admin.

## What changes when you have one

Your next page load and your socios' next reply follow the admins' choice. A socio only offers what you can use: when a feature is off for you, its tools are not in your chats, so a socio won't use them or suggest them.

Something wrong with an early-access feature? Report it on the improvements board, mentioning the feature's name.
