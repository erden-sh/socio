## Check the thing you are sharing

| Material                                           | Normal visibility                                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A personal socio                                   | Its owner; admins retain settings-management authority                                                     |
| A workspace socio                                  | People in the workspace                                                                                    |
| A private chat, including a page conversation      | Its owner; a page conversation only while they can see the page's Space                                    |
| Spaces and pages                                   | People in the workspace; a private Space's people and workspace admins only                                |
| Your personal files, datasets and memories         | You, with specific grants and sharing where supported                                                      |
| Your episodes (what socios keep of your own chats) | You and your socios, in your own chats only; never in a chat with other people in it                       |
| A Space's shared memory (approved entries)         | Everyone who can read the Space; in a room, only when every member can                                     |
| A Telegram team topic                              | People who can read it under Telegram group membership and settings, including members not linked to Socio |
| Learning proposals and their evidence              | Admins reviewing Learning                                                                                  |

A shared socio does not share its users' private chats. A shared page does not share each person's conversation about that page. A personal socio does not turn a Space page into a private document.

Private describes access through Socio. Your hosting operators control the deployment and its backups; personal content is not a separate vault from that infrastructure.

## Personal is the creation default

New socios start personal for members and admins. Admins can explicitly choose **Share with the workspace** during creation. The form explains workspace sharing, and a saved socio shows a **Personal** or **Workspace** badge. Existing workspace socios keep their ownership. Editing settings does not convert ownership.

Admins can manage personal socio settings intentionally. Another person's personal chat, history, runs and computer status are still subject to owner access. Treat “not found” as an access boundary, not a reason to ask a socio to bypass it.

## Computers and personal data are different

A workspace socio's computer includes shared browser state and a shared working area. Use it as a team resource. Current computer shell/file operations also have private per-person homes; explicitly shared files belong under `shared/`. These protections do not make shared browser sessions personal. For sensitive browser activity, choose a personal socio and check your team's policies.

Media and Data belong to your personal data space. Grant a socio only the folders or datasets it needs. Copying material into a shared computer area or sharing a dataset with a Space changes who can use it: everyone who can see that Space. Deleting a file hides it from normal use but does not immediately purge retained bytes or backups.

## Models, tools and Learning

Prompts and relevant material reach the configured model provider. External tools may send the arguments you approve to another service. Read the request before approving it; using a personal socio does not keep a provider from receiving work you send there.

When a socio is assigned a Learning scope, eligible finished conversations can contribute evidence. Admins review proposals and evidence; members see published skills under **Skills**. Redaction and review reduce exposure, but do not treat Learning as a private vault. Ask an admin about scope settings before using especially sensitive material.

Review [data and media](/docs/data-media), [approvals](/docs/approvals) and [connected agents](/docs/connected-agents) when changing access.
