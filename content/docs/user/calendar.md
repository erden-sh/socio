title: 'Calendar'
description: 'See and add events on the shared calendar, and subscribe to it from your calendar app.'
---

When an admin has installed the calendar for you, **Calendar** is in the sidebar. It shows the team's events by week, month or as an agenda, in the calendar's time zone, which the page names. On a phone it opens on the agenda.

## Add and change events

Choose a time on the week grid or a day in the month, or use **New event**. An event has a title, a start and an end (or a whole day or days), a location, a description and a status: **Confirmed**, **Hold** or **Canceled**. **Only me** keeps an event private: nobody else sees it, admins included.

You can change and delete the events you created. Drag an event to move it; it keeps its length. An event someone else created opens read-only unless your admin lets everyone edit. Canceled events are hidden unless you turn on **Show canceled**.

## Ask a socio or an agent

Your socios can list, add, change and delete events for you, with your permissions. So can an agent you connect to Socio, once you approve the calendar's permissions on the consent screen. An event added this way says which socio or agent added it.

## Subscribe from your calendar app

**Subscribe** makes a private link to paste into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook. It shows the events you can see, from three months back to a year ahead, and your app refreshes it on its own schedule. Anyone with the link can read those events, so keep it to yourself. Making a new link ends the old one, and **Turn off** ends it without a new one.
