title: 'Socios'
description: 'Choose a specialist and understand personal versus workspace assistants.'

A socio is a specialist with a name, a Soul, authorized Spaces and optional tools. Choose one for the work you want it to do. Its Soul explains its role and how it works; its permissions determine what it can actually read or change.

## Create a personal socio

Use **Create socio**, give it a recognizable name and describe its role. Select its default Space and review the available permissions before saving. New socios are personal by default, including when an admin or operator creates one. Members create personal socios and do not have a workspace-sharing switch.

Admins can explicitly enable **Share with the workspace** during creation. The form changes its explanation to Workspace socio. This choice matters: other people can use the socio, and its computer has shared facilities. Do not share one merely to let a colleague read a document; the [Space and page](/docs/spaces) may already be shared.

A saved socio's **Personal** or **Workspace** badge shows its visibility. Personal socios appear for their owner; workspace socios appear for everyone who can use them. Existing workspace socios stay shared after an upgrade. Editing a socio does not convert its ownership between personal and workspace.

## Its home page

Select a socio in the sidebar to open its home page: your chats with it, its turns, approvals waiting for you, what it filed on the board, and its goals and schedules. **Apps** lists the mini apps it built that you can already open: your own, and those shared with a Space you belong to. Each is marked **Published** (a workspace app), **In development** (it runs only while the socio's computer runs it) or **Unpublished**. Selecting an app opens it in a new tab; that does not start a stopped app or its computer. An unpublished app links to the **Apps** page, where its owner can publish it again. A personal socio's home page, and so its apps, is visible only to its owner.

## Its Soul

A socio's **Soul** is its persona: its role and standing instructions, and optionally its voice (how it sounds), values (what it weighs) and working style (how it works with you). Open it from the socio's home page or its menu with **Soul**. Anyone who can use a socio can read its Soul and its history.

- **Who edits:** the owner edits a personal socio's Soul; admins edit a workspace socio's Soul. Nobody else does, and a socio never changes its own Soul or another's.
- **Versions:** every save is a new version. **History** lists them all, newest first, with who made each one and when. **Changes** shows what each version changed from the one before. **Restore** puts an earlier version back as a new version, so nothing is lost. An archived socio's Soul is read-only until it is unarchived.
- **Preview:** while editing, **Preview** shows the text the socio reads.
- **When it applies:** the Soul is part of every chat with the socio, from its next turn. It changes only when someone edits or restores it, never on its own from a conversation.
- **Its limits:** the Soul shapes tone and role. It never overrides Socio's rules, the socio's permissions or [approvals](/docs/approvals).

When you create a socio, its role instructions become version 1 of its Soul. Socios made before the Soul existed kept their role instructions, word for word, as version 1.

## Choose a character

Each socio has a character from the Socio cast: Sebas the stone crab, Manny the manatee, Mingo the flamingo or Rulo the curly-tailed lizard. Pick one under **Character** when you create a socio. The form suggests one that none of your socios uses yet, but several socios can share a character. The character is how the socio looks everywhere: the sidebar, its home, chats, calls and notifications.

To change it later, open the socio's settings and pick another character. Whoever can edit a socio's settings can change its character: the owner of a personal socio, and admins for any socio. A socio nobody has picked a character for keeps the look it always had, including after an upgrade. Each change is recorded in the socio's audit, which admins can review.

## Spaces and capabilities

The default Space is a page destination. Additional authorized Spaces allow the socio to work with those documents. A page conversation uses the page's own Space. A socio cannot infer access from a page link or a request in chat: an appropriate permission must exist.

Computer browser, file and shell permissions begin disabled. If your team has a computer service, only enabled capabilities are available. A computer's files and browser state are separate from a conversation and from your personal Media files. See [privacy](/docs/privacy) before using shared computers for sensitive work.

## Manage and retire

Owners can change the permitted settings of their personal socios; admins retain management authority, including over personal socio settings. That authority does not make them the owner of your private chats or personal Data namespace. Some settings remain admin-controlled.

Archive a socio when it is no longer needed rather than reusing its identity for a different purpose. Ask an admin about workspace socios or broader permissions. A new Soul does not bypass [approvals](/docs/approvals), model availability or access checks.
