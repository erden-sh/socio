---
title: 'Ask a socio'
description: 'Ask a socio about the page, issue, dataset or schedule on your screen without leaving it.'
---

**Ask a socio** is a panel you can open on every screen. It keeps your conversation beside what you are looking at, so you can ask a question about it and carry on working.

## Open the panel

- Select the question-mark speech bubble at the top right of any screen.
- Press **⌘J** on a Mac, or **Ctrl+J** elsewhere. Press it again to close the panel.
- Or open search with **⌘K** or **Ctrl+K** and choose **Ask a socio about this screen**.

On a wide screen the panel opens beside the view and stays open while you scroll, edit or click in the view. Press **Escape** or **Close** to put it away; the keyboard focus goes back where it was. On a phone it opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen.

## What the socio sees

The panel shows what it is about, under its title:

- a **page** you have open;
- an **issue** on the [Improvements board](/docs/improvements);
- one of your **datasets** (its name, description, tables and columns, never its rows);
- one of your **schedules**.

On any other screen the panel is a quick conversation about nothing in particular.

The socio sees the item as **you** can see it, read again with every message you send. If the item is deleted, or you lose access to it, the socio is told that it is gone and is shown nothing more of it. For a page, the socio also needs access to the page's Space: only socios that have it appear in the list. What the socio can do beyond reading the item still depends on its own tools and permissions; see [socios](/docs/socios).

## Choose a socio

The panel starts with the socio you asked last. Pick another in the **Socio** list. Your own socios are marked **(yours)**: nobody else can see or use them. Each socio has its own conversation with you about each item, so changing the socio switches to that conversation, or a new one.

## Your conversation is yours

A panel conversation belongs to you, like any other [chat](/docs/chats). It appears in your chats, titled after the item, and other people who ask about the same item have their own conversations. Asking about a shared page doesn't change the page unless you ask the socio to edit it. A page's own conversation, at the bottom of the page, is a separate conversation; see [page conversations](/docs/page-conversations).

Read [privacy](/docs/privacy) before you paste personal material into a conversation about shared work.
