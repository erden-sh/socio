title: 'Approvals'
description: 'Decide what a socio may do and review what happened afterwards.'
---

Some tools propose an action before performing it. The request appears in chat and in **Approvals**, under **Waiting for you**. Approval is tied to the action described there, the socio and the person it acts for.

## Read before deciding

Review the summary and details, especially destinations, recipients, changed data, external service arguments and recurring work. Approve only the action you intend. Deny a request that is wrong, too broad or no longer useful, then explain the desired correction in the conversation.

An approved request can still fail when it executes. Look at its final status and outcome; approval is not proof that the work succeeded. Expired or denied requests do not authorize a later changed action. A turn can continue after your decision, so return to the conversation for the result.

## Policies and automatic decisions

The **Policies** section sets how a particular socio and capability asks you. Manual review is the safest starting point. A bypass skips that question; use it only for a capability you understand. Where available, automatic review asks a model to judge a request using your guidance and the capability's rules. High risk, unavailable models, limits or other guards can escalate it to you.

Use **Guidance for auto approvals** for standing preferences, and review **Recent decisions** and **Audit** to see what actually happened. Automatic review is a model decision, not a guarantee. Undo is offered only for actions that support it; many real-world effects cannot be reversed by Socio.

## External MCP tools

External tool calls require manual approval every time. They cannot be made automatic through a general policy and cannot be undone here. Read the external arguments and provider before approving. An interrupted call is not automatically repeated; check the provider if the outcome is uncertain before requesting it again.

## Socios using apps for you

Where the feature is on, a socio can ask to use a mini app for you. Its card names the app, its owner and address, the access (read, read and change, or ask to act), for how long, and what it can never do. The socio's own words come last, marked as its. Write access and acting always ask you. Under **Settings → Account → Socio access** you can let a socio you trust read apps without asking, and end any access you gave.

With act access, each action that sends, spends or deletes still asks you, on a card with the exact parameters the app will use. Allow runs that one call, once. If the parameters change, you're asked again.

## New Spaces

A socio that makes a Space for you always asks first, whatever your policies say. The card shows the Space's name, its description and who will see it. Approving makes the Space as if you made it yourself and gives the socio access to it. See [Spaces](/docs/spaces).

## Scope still applies

Approval does not grant a socio a missing Space, folder, computer permission or another person's account. Data sharing, new datasets, schedules, goals and saved job versions each have their own boundaries. Learn more in [Data and media](/docs/data-media), [schedules](/docs/schedules) and [connected agents](/docs/connected-agents).
