title: 'Rooms (preview)'
description: 'Share one conversation with several people and a company socio.'
---

Rooms are a preview: they work, and they may still change. A room is one conversation for several people and a company socio. Everyone in it reads the same messages and writes to the same socio. When an admin has turned Rooms on (**Settings → General → Rooms**), **Rooms** appears in your sidebar, under your recent chats. Otherwise the sidebar has no Rooms section, and you can't create, share or join a room.

## Start a room

Select **+** next to **Rooms**, name the room and choose a company socio. You become the room's admin, and the room's **Members** panel opens for you to invite people. Personal socios can't be in rooms.

You can also share one of your own chats as a room. Open the chat's **⋯** menu and select **Share as a room…**. Everyone you invite will read the whole chat, including what you wrote before it became a room. Check the box to confirm that before you continue. The chat keeps its link and history, and you become its admin. You can't share a chat with a personal socio, a Telegram chat, an archived chat, or a chat that has a call or scheduled tasks.

## Join or decline

An invitation shows on your home page and under **Rooms → Invitations**. It says who invited you, how many people are in the room and how long the invitation lasts (7 days). Joining gives you the room's whole shared history, including messages from before you joined. Everyone in the room reads what you write there. Check **I understand what joining shares**, then select **Join room**. **Decline** turns the invitation down. You see none of the room's messages until you join.

## Talk in a room

Each message shows who wrote it. Other people's messages show their initials and name, your own show **You**, and each reply shows the name of the socio that wrote it, with a **socio** tag. Names never decide who someone is: the server records each sender, and two people with the same name get differently colored avatars. In a room that was someone's chat, the messages from before it became a room show as theirs.

When you send a message, the socio answers it acting as you. It uses your access and approvals, and your memories only if you marked them **Use in team chats**. It never uses another member's. If the socio is already answering someone, your message waits in the room's queue, where you can **Edit** or **Cancel** it until it is read. Messages are answered in the order they were sent, one person's at a time. **Stop** ends a reply. Only the person who asked, or a room admin, can stop one. Stopping a reply never drops the messages still waiting: they go next, in order. Your draft is kept for you per room, in this browser only, and nobody else sees it. A message the room refuses (too many messages a minute, a full queue) stays in the box.

Your queued messages are yours: other members don't see them until the socio reads them, and only you can edit them. Room admins also see everyone's waiting messages and can cancel one, for example if its writer has left the room, but they can't change what it says. The room's audit records who canceled whose message. Once the socio has read a message, it can't be edited or canceled any more.

## Mention someone

Type **@** and the start of a name: a list of the room's other members opens. Use the arrow keys and **Enter** or **Tab**, or click a name, to put it in your message. **Escape** closes the list. Only people in the room are listed, never anyone outside it. Two people with the same name show twice, each with the avatar color the room gives them, and the one you pick is the one mentioned. A name you type without picking it from the list mentions nobody, and neither does a name you paste or quote. Once the socio has read your message, the person you mentioned gets a notification, and the message shows **@ you** to them.

## Notifications

A room tells you when someone mentions you, when a reply to your own message arrives while you're away, and when a long job you started reports back. It never tells you about your own mentions or anything while you're looking at the room. While you're elsewhere in Socio it shows in the app; otherwise it goes to the channels you turned on in **Settings → Account → Notifications** (this device, Telegram), showing the message or only that something needs you, as you chose there. The notification shows what was written in the room, never a tool's result, and opens the room.

Choose how much each room tells you in its **⋯** menu, under **Notify me about**: **Everything** (also each reply to someone else, once per message), **Mentions and my turns** (the default) or **Muted** (nothing). A muted room still counts what's new, quietly, with a crossed bell in the sidebar. You hear about an invitation to a room whatever you chose. Once you leave or are removed, a room tells you nothing more.

## Who's here

The room's header shows the avatars of the other members looking at it now. Someone shows while the room is open in front of them and they used it in the last minute, and disappears a few seconds after they switch away or close it. Only the room's members see it, and only members are ever shown. To look without showing, turn off **Show when I'm in a room** in **Settings → Account → Notifications → Rooms**.

## Unread messages

When someone else writes in a room and the socio answers, the room's name turns bold in the sidebar, with a count of the new replies beside it. Your own messages never count, but the report of a long job you started does, since it arrives later. Opening the room clears it, on all your devices. A room with something new shows in the sidebar even when it is past the first five.

Other people's messages appear as their turn starts, and the socio's reply as it writes it. While the socio works on someone else's message you see only that it is thinking, never its reasoning, which can retell what that person's own access found. If the socio can't answer a message (the person may not work in the room now, a limit was reached, or the reply failed), a note under the message says so, for everyone in the room: you see what Socio told you, and the others see why your message wasn't answered. If the live connection drops, the room keeps updating every few seconds until it's back.

## Pinned facts

Open **Pinned** from the room's header to see the facts the room keeps in front of its socio. The socio reads every pinned fact with every message, word for word, so decisions, goals and names stay current however long the room gets.

- **Admins** pin a fact at once, edit or unpin any of them, and approve or turn down what others propose.
- **Members** propose a fact, or suggest a change to a pinned one. It waits under **Proposed** until an admin decides. You can **Withdraw** your own proposal while it waits.
- Whoever pinned a fact (including a member whose proposal was approved) can edit or unpin it too.

Each fact shows who pinned it and when. **History** lists every version: who proposed, approved, edited, replaced or unpinned it, and when. A room keeps at most 30 pinned facts and 6,000 characters in all. Unpin one to make room.

When a room gets long, its socio keeps notes on the earlier messages for everyone in it, with who said what. The **Pinned** panel shows them with their versions, and you can pin or propose one of their facts. The notes never hold what a socio found with someone's own access, and nothing from a private chat, a memory or a person's earlier chats is pinned or used there.

## Members and roles

Open **Members** from the room's header, or from **⋯** next to the room. Everyone sees who is in the room, who is invited, each person's role and the room's socios.

- **Admins** invite people (as members or admins), take an invitation back, make someone an admin or a member, remove people, rename or archive the room, choose its socios and lower its handoffs.
- **Members** read, write and leave.

To leave, select **Leave room**. A room always keeps an admin. If you are the last one, choose who takes the role over, and you leave once they have it. If nobody else is in the room, archive it instead.

## Socios in a room

The room's **lead** socio answers. A room admin can ask for another company socio. It joins once a workspace admin lets it in (**Settings → General → Rooms**, or the room's Members panel). The room admin can then make it the lead. Workspace admins can take a socio out of any room they are in.

### Several socios

When a room has more than one socio, each reply shows the name of the socio that wrote it.

- **Ask a socio by name.** Select **@** at the bottom of the message box and pick a socio, or type `@` and the socio's name. The line under the box says who the message goes to before you send it. A message that names no socio goes to the lead. A message that names several goes to each of them, one after another, in the order you named them. Mentioning a person doesn't send the message to a socio, and a name that is a socio's in this room always means the socio.
- **Handoffs.** A socio can hand your message's work to another socio in the room when that socio is better placed. The room shows a line such as "Atlas handed this to Ledger: Ledger knows the billing rules", and the other socio carries on.

Whichever socio answers, it acts as you: your access, your approvals, and only the memories you marked **Use in team chats**. A handoff never switches to another member's or the socio owner's access. **Stop** ends the reply and anything that was to follow it.

Handoffs are limited. One message's work can pass between socios 4 times by default, and a socio never hands the same work to the same socio twice, so socios can't bounce work back and forth. Each further socio a message names counts as one handoff too. An admin sets the limit under **Settings → General → Work per message**. A room admin can lower it for their room under **Members → Socios → Handoffs per message**, but never raise it. When the limit is reached, the socio answers itself and says what is left.

In a linked Telegram topic (below), the lead answers every message: naming a socio and handoffs work in the app only, for now.

## Link a Telegram topic

A room admin can link the room to a forum topic in the team's Telegram group, so its members can also talk to the socio there. Open the room's **⋯** menu and select **Telegram topic**. You need to be an admin of the Telegram group too, and to have linked your Telegram account. Get a code and send it in the topic; the bot confirms there. Everyone in the Telegram group can read the topic, including people who don't use Socio, so the dialog asks you to confirm that first.

In the topic, only the room's members are answered, each as themselves. The dialog lists the people the bot didn't answer, and linked people seen in the group, so you can invite them. Nobody joins the room unless they accept an invitation. Archiving the room unlinks the topic. See [Telegram](/docs/telegram#topics-linked-to-a-room).

## Archive and Rooms off

A room admin archives a room from its **⋯** menu. An archived room is kept: its members can still read it, nothing new runs in it, and it moves to **Settings → Account → Archived chats**. Its admins can unarchive it from there or from the room. Rooms are never deleted.

If an admin turns Rooms off, the Rooms section and invitations disappear from the app. A room's link still opens it read-only.

## Privacy

Joining a room never shares your private chats, memories, files or connections. What a socio did with another member's own access stays theirs, and so does its reasoning in their turns. A workspace admin's role doesn't give them access to a room's messages. See [Privacy](/docs/privacy) and [Chats](/docs/chats).
