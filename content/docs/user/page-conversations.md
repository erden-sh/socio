---
title: 'Page conversations'
description: 'Ask a specialist to help with a shared document while keeping your chat personal.'
---

A page conversation keeps the saved document beside your conversation with a socio. It is useful for explaining a page, finding gaps, drafting a revision or creating related pages. It uses the same model and tool permissions as that socio's other work for you.

## Start beside the document

1. Open the page in its Space.
2. Save any manual edits and wait for the save status.
3. Open the page's conversation and select a specialist with access to the Space.
4. Explain the task: for example, “Find unclear steps in this checklist before changing it.”

Socio creates or reuses your conversation for that page and specialist. The current saved page becomes context. The page's Space is the default destination for pages created from this conversation; the socio can use other Spaces only where authorized.

## Review changes

Be explicit about whether you want advice or an edit. Read a proposed change before accepting it when review is offered. The server checks revisions so a stale edit cannot silently replace newer content. If another edit conflicts, keep your draft and compare it with the saved page rather than repeatedly submitting the old version.

A model connection is needed for a reply, but not for your manual page editing. If a specialist is unavailable, check its Space access and inference availability. Selecting another specialist can open a different conversation; it does not transfer an earlier specialist's whole conversation to it.

## Understand the boundary

The page is shared workspace knowledge. Your page conversation belongs to you, and other people have their own conversations about that same page. Saving a change updates the shared document. Discussing personal material in the chat does not automatically publish it, but asking to put that material into the page does.

The **Ask a socio** panel at the top of every screen is a separate conversation of yours about the page; see [Ask a socio](/docs/ask-a-socio).

Read [pages](/docs/pages) for saving and conflict recovery, [Spaces](/docs/spaces) for document organization, and [privacy](/docs/privacy) before copying chat content into shared knowledge.
